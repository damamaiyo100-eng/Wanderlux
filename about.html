About WanderLux

Turning travel dreams into extraordinary realities since 2012 – with passion, expertise and personal care.

📖 Our Story

From Passion to Purpose

WanderLux was founded in 2012 by a group of passionate travellers who believed that exceptional travel experiences shouldn't be reserved for the privileged few. Starting with a small office in Melbourne's CBD, we've grown into one of Australia's most trusted independent travel agencies.

Over the past 12 years, we have helped more than 15,000 clients explore 80+ destinations across six continents – from budget backpacker adventures to ultra-luxury honeymoons and everything in between.

Our philosophy is simple: every traveller is unique, and every journey should be tailored to reflect that. We don't just book flights and hotels – we craft stories worth telling.
